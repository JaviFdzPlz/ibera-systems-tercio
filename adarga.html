TERCIO / INDIVIDUAL TERMINAL SURVIVAL

ADARGAA feasibility-led terminal-survival concept.

ADARGA is the TERCIO concept for individual terminal survival in the final moments before an already-committed threat reaches its target, explored here as a feasibility-level concept.

01 / OPERATIONAL PROBLEM

Some intervals are too short for any earlier layer.

URCE, GADIR, ATALA, ARDID and CID address signature, concealment, warning, deception and stand-off response. None of them can guarantee that every threat is prevented from reaching final commitment. ADARGA addresses the residual, individual-scale terminal interval as a separate and bounded problem.

This is deliberately treated as a feasibility question, not a product claim: whether a passive or human-controlled individual-scale concept could plausibly improve terminal outcomes without introducing new hazards of its own.

02 / CAPABILITY LOGIC & BOUNDARY

Individual scale.
Human-controlled by design.

ADARGA is distinct from GADIR (position-level signature management) and URCE (worn signature conditioning): it addresses the individual soldier's terminal interval specifically. Any activation logic considered would be evaluated first against human-factors, safety and false-activation risk, not against a performance target.

Boundary rule: ADARGA does not claim automatic engagement authority, guaranteed survival or demonstrated terminal protection. Any human-control, activation or safety model remains a feasibility question until it is separately developed, tested and evidenced.

03 / DEVELOPMENT POSTURE

Feasibility study before hardware.

ADARGA is currently at feasibility-study level — no test programme, performance figure or committed development pathway exists yet. Candidate MVP/demonstrator opportunities across all seven TERCIO systems, including ADARGA, carry equal default priority at this stage.

The near-term objective is a bounded feasibility study covering candidate mechanisms, human-factors and safety constraints, and an explicit human-control model, conducted with qualified human-factors and safety partners before any physical concept is proposed publicly.

04 / DEVELOP ADARGA WITH US

Study feasibility and safety before defining a device.

ÍBERA Systems is seeking human-factors, safety-engineering and protective-equipment partners to help scope a bounded ADARGA feasibility study, with false-activation risk and user safety as first-order design constraints.

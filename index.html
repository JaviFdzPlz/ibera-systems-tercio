Working logic

MILLARES starts from the threat and the protection required. We examine different ways of responding, challenge what we think we know and engineer the response that makes sense for the problem — without assuming its form in advance.

As the threat changes, we reassess the protection and the engineering direction.

01 / About

Defence research and engineering for a moving protection problem.

MILLARES is a defence research and engineering initiative focused on protective problems whose threat picture, evidence and operating conditions can change while an engineering response is still being developed.

02 / Why

Protection can be overtaken by the threat.

Changes in how warfare observes, decides, coordinates and attacks can alter the protection problem while engineering commitments are already under way.

The challenge is not simply that threats evolve. Protective approaches can remain anchored to assumptions, categories or operating conditions that were reasonable when development began but no longer describe the problem that matters.

MILLARES keeps returning to that mismatch: what has changed, what protection is now required, and whether the engineering direction still fits.

01 / ChangeThreat mechanisms change

Observation, coordination, attack methods and tactics can combine in new ways.

02 / ConditionsConditions change

Mission, terrain, signatures, connectivity and constraints can alter what protection needs to achieve.

03 / CommitmentEngineering commits

Real decisions consume time and resources and become harder to reverse as development progresses.

The response should follow the current problem, not the previous assumption.

03 / What

From threat understanding to an engineered response.

MILLARES works from structured threat understanding towards materially different protective responses, then narrows only when the case for an engineering direction is strong enough.

Today, the public site is not a finished capability or product catalogue. A capability direction, a candidate architecture and evidence that a real implementation performs are different things, and we do not present one as proof of another.

Engineering destination

Analysis is not the destination. Engineering is.

The result may be a new system, an adaptation, an integration, a service or another engineered response. The point is not to keep the outcome abstract; it is to avoid deciding the form before the problem has earned that commitment.

People, platforms, assets and infrastructure define the protection scope. They are not product divisions and do not predetermine the architecture of the response.

04 / How we work

Start with what can defeat the protection.

We begin with the threat, what needs protecting and the conditions in which that protection has to work. We compare alternatives before commitment, challenge the assumptions that matter and keep the decision traceable enough to reopen when its basis changes.

Different problems may need different models, tools, specialist inputs and experiments. No model is treated as engineering authority. Material commitments remain accountable human decisions.

Observe

Let new evidence change the question.

Operational observations and empirical evidence can force us to reconsider what the protection problem actually is, not just tune a preferred answer.

Challenge

Use models as tools, not as reality.

Analysis, simulation and AI-assisted work can expose weak assumptions, compare alternatives and generate questions. Agreement between models is not evidence of physical effectiveness.

Test

Run work that can change the engineering decision.

Experiments and trials matter most when their outcomes can discriminate between meaningful alternatives, reject a weak direction or justify commitment.

R

Why repeat?

The threat can change after the decision.

Choosing an engineering direction does not make the assumptions behind it permanently true. If the threat, operating conditions or available evidence change materially, we return to the question that matters: does the protection still fit the problem?

If it does, the commitment stands. If it does not, the response needs to be reconsidered.

Working with others

As work develops, MILLARES may need operational, scientific, engineering, industrial, manufacturing, integration, test or other specialist capability. We seek the expertise the problem justifies. External expertise does not replace evidence or accountable engineering judgement, and this site does not imply named partnerships that have not been publicly established.

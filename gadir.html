TERCIO / L1 / PASSIVE POSITION SURVIVABILITY

GADIRConceal. Protect. Remain operational.

GADIR is the TERCIO capability focused on always-on, non-powered position concealment and residual-effect mitigation. It is intended to preserve options before detection and retain passive protection after penetration.

01 / OPERATIONAL PROBLEM

Protection must begin before engagement.

Thermal surveillance and short-range FPV attack compress the interval between observation, targeting and effect. Position survivability cannot depend solely on active sensing, jamming or centralized protection.

GADIR addresses this gap through passive material, geometry and standoff concepts intended to reduce stable signature, complicate observation and preserve a measure of residual protection without continuous power or network dependence.

02 / CAPABILITY LOGIC

One capability.
Two bounded pathways.

GADIR is the capability layer. GADIR-TB and GADIR-FS are candidate demonstrator pathways, each built around a distinct development question while remaining consistent with the same passive-first design basis.

03 / CANDIDATE MVP PATHWAYS

CURRENT LEAD PATH

GADIR-TB

Passive position signature-management screen using candidate material and maintained-standoff configurations. The development pathway compares uncovered, baseline-camouflage and candidate configurations under controlled LWIR observation.

Development focus: relative contour stability, apparent contrast behaviour, deployment burden, durability, repairability and thermal burden.
SECOND PATHWAY

GADIR-FS

Passive overhead and residual-effect protection concept intended to preserve physical protection when detection or deception fails. The initial pathway examines deployable geometry, coverage, mass, setup burden and repeatability.

Development focus: structural concept, deployment sequence, coverage envelope, repairability and safe test design.

04 / REPRESENTATIVE TEST LOGIC

Measure against controls.
Build evidence by design.

The GADIR development route uses defined baselines, repeatable geometry, environmental recording and independent observation. Candidate configurations are compared with uncovered and conventional camouflage controls.

ÍBERA Systems provides the system architecture, programme framework and integration authority, working with specialist partners to mature each tested critical function while preserving disciplined separation between subsystem evidence and the wider TERCIO architecture.
